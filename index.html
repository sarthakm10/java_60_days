How it works

A day, in four steps

Two to three hours a day. Everything you need for the day is on one page.

  1. 1 📖

    Read the notes

    Plain-language explanations first, with diagrams and runnable examples. Deeper details wait in folded panels for a second pass.

  2. 2 ⌨️

    Code it yourself

    Exercises with starter code, hints one at a time, and a reference solution. Run them in your own IDE, just like on the job.

  3. 3 ✅

    Tick it off

    Check off each topic as you learn it. Your streak, XP, level and badges grow with you, and a heatmap shows every day you studied.

  4. 4 🧠

    Revise at the right time

    Topics you tick come back after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days, and flashcards are built from every day's notes, so nothing fades.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything (notes, exercises, flashcards, question sets and tracking) is free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Do I need an account? Where is my progress saved?

No account. Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. Use Export in the app's sidebar to download a backup file, and Import to restore it or move it to another device. Clearing your browser's site data erases progress you haven't exported.

I've never coded before. Is that really OK?

Yes. That's exactly who the Beginner track is for. Day 1 explains what programming is and walks you through installing Java and an editor. Nothing is assumed; each day builds on the ones before.

How much time does it take each day?

About two to three hours on most days, and three to four on heavier ones. Going slower is completely fine: the app tracks what you've done, not the calendar.

What do I need to install?

To start: Java 21 (the JDK) and a code editor such as IntelliJ IDEA Community, both free. Later phases add PostgreSQL, Maven (via the project's wrapper) and Docker, each introduced on the day you need it.

I already know Java. Where should I start?

Take the 60-day Interview track: it assumes you know the language and goes straight to interview depth. You can also open any Beginner day directly to fill gaps.

Can I switch tracks later?

Any time, from the switch at the top of the app's sidebar (or the 🌱/🎯 button on phones). Each track keeps its own progress, so nothing is lost.
